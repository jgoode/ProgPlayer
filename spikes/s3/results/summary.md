## Notes on the quality metric

- The decoded stream can be offset from the source by the encoder's algorithmic delay, so the alignment lag is found first by cross-correlating a 4096-sample window over ±4096 samples, and the SNR is computed at that lag. Without this step the measurement reports the delay as noise and is meaningless.
- A lag of 0 means the encoder writes a LAME-style gapless Info/Xing tag, so the decoder trims the delay. A non-zero lag means it does not, and the decoded file starts late.
- The band split compares error energy in the lower and upper half of the time-ordered sample stream. It is a coarse pre-echo / cut-off indicator, not a spectral measurement.
- Decoding uses `mpg123-decoder` (mpg123 compiled to WASM).

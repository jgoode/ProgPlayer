- [x] P1.3 Spike S3: MP3 encoder licensing and quality — **done 2026-11-06, WAV-only in v1.0** (ADR-0004). Eight npm-distributable candidates plus four Rust crates surveyed; **every npm/WASM encoder embeds a copyleft codec** (`wasm-media-encoders` MIT/**LGPL LAME**, `@mediabunny/mp3-encoder` **MPL-2.0**/**LGPL LAME 3.100**, `@audio/encode-mp3` MIT/LGPL LAME, `@breezystack/lamejs` **LGPL-3.0**, `@toots/shine.js` **LGPL-2.0**, `@etercast/mp3` **GPL-3.0**), and the LGPL relinking condition is not demonstrable for a static WASM/JS bundle. A 5-minute stereo render was encoded at 44.1/48 kHz and 128/192 kbps in Node and in a real Chromium renderer: **every working candidate ran at 31×–107× real time**, so speed was never the constraint. `rusty_mp3` (Apache-2.0, pure Rust) was the only combined-permissive encoder and also measured best on quality (SNR 35.2 dB at 128 kbps / 39.6 dB at 192 kbps vs 25.9/30.4 dB for the LAME builds) — but it has no npm/WASM distribution. **Nothing was added to `src/`.** Harness: `spikes/s3/`; report: `docs/spikes/s3-mp3-encoder-report.md`
  - **The licence is the blocker, not speed.** Every candidate ran at 31×–107× real time, far above FR-IO-4's "faster than real time" requirement. The finding is that **a wrapper's licence does not override the embedded codec's**, so the MIT-licensed wrappers (`wasm-media-encoders`, `@audio/encode-mp3`) still ship LGPL LAME.
  - **MP3 patents are not the constraint** (expired: EU/most jurisdictions by end-2012, US 2017-04-16, Japan 2017-04-23), so a future MP3 decision is a pure licence-and-quality decision.
  - **`rusty_mp3` is the designated candidate if MP3 is reopened**, subject to a human listening test (G-24) and a project-owned WASM build (G-25).
  - **Two measurement bugs were found and fixed**: the quality metric did not remove the encoder's algorithmic delay (it now cross-correlates the lag first), and bundling `mediabunny` and its encoder package separately broke the encoder registration (they must share one bundle).
